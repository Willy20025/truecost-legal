1. Who operates TrueCost

TrueCost is operated by IRA Canada (Integration Rapide en Action – Canada), operated by Willy Uwera, Sole Proprietor. Contact: uwwilly1@gmail.com.

2. Account information

TrueCost uses Firebase Authentication and supports email/password authentication and Google Sign-In. TrueCost application code does not directly store Firebase passwords in TrueCost local application storage or send them to the TrueCost backend. Firebase user identifiers and authentication tokens may be used for authenticated functionality. Google and Firebase process information under their own policies.

3. Calculator and local app data

Users may provide financial facts such as prices, balances, rates, payments, terms, taxes, premiums, deductibles, coverage amounts, and similar inputs. Primary financial calculations are deterministic. Saved scenarios and app preferences may be stored locally. TrueCost does not collect online-banking credentials for bank aggregation.

4. Documents and text recognition

Supported documents and images can be selected or captured for analysis. Google ML Kit performs text recognition on the device. Original imported documents may be stored in TrueCost private application storage so they can be reopened. The current client does not send raw original documents or raw OCR/native page text to the TrueCost backend. Eligible verified text intended for backend evidence is subject to privacy filtering. Automated privacy filtering and redaction reduce risk but cannot guarantee perfect detection or removal; review information before relying on it.

5. Backend and third-party features

Authenticated functionality may communicate with the TrueCost backend. Free-form Ask AI/OpenAI functionality is dormant and inaccessible in the current release interface. Bank of Canada and OpenAI support may exist in source code but is not presented here as active user functionality. This release contains no dedicated advertising, behavioural-advertising, payment-processing, bank-aggregation, analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising-attribution SDKs.

6. Device permissions and backup

The current Android release does not request location, microphone, contacts, SMS, phone, calendar, or broad-storage permission. It may use system document/image selection and camera capture through system providers. TrueCost application data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer by the app configuration.

7. Storage, deletion, and security

Local documents and saved scenarios remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears app data/uninstalls the app, or successfully deletes the TrueCost account. Successful in-app account deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account and then deletes TrueCost-owned imported documents, saved scenarios, related metadata, preferences, and session state stored locally on that device. It does not delete records controlled independently by third parties. See the account-deletion instructions. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes and questions

This policy may change when TrueCost’s practices or legal obligations change. The effective date above identifies this version. Questions may be sent to uwwilly1@gmail.com.

Les utilisateurs peuvent fournir des données financières telles que prix, soldes, taux, paiements, durées, taxes, primes, franchises, montants de garantie et données semblables. Des documents et images pris en charge peuvent être sélectionnés ou capturés pour analyse. Google ML Kit effectue la reconnaissance de texte sur l’appareil. Le texte vérifié admissible destiné aux éléments probants du serveur fait l’objet d’un filtrage de confidentialité. Le filtrage et le masquage automatisés réduisent le risque sans garantir une détection ou suppression parfaite; vérifiez l’information avant de vous y fier.

La version Android actuelle ne demande pas d’autorisation d’accès à la localisation, au microphone, aux contacts, aux SMS, au téléphone, au calendrier ou au stockage général. Elle peut utiliser les sélecteurs système de documents/images et la capture photo par les fournisseurs système. La configuration exclut les données de TrueCost de la sauvegarde infonuagique Android et du transfert d’appareil à appareil.

7. Conservation, suppression et sécurité

Les documents locaux et scénarios enregistrés restent sur l’appareil jusqu’à leur suppression par l’utilisateur, l’effacement des données/la désinstallation, ou la suppression réussie du compte TrueCost. La suppression réussie dans l’application supprime le compte Firebase Authentication, puis les documents importés, scénarios enregistrés, métadonnées connexes, préférences et données de session appartenant à TrueCost sur cet appareil. Elle ne supprime pas les dossiers contrôlés indépendamment par des tiers. Consultez les instructions de suppression. Aucun système ne peut garantir une sécurité absolue.
